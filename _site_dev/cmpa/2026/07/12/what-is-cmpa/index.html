    What is CMPA? Our Journey to Diagnosis

    July 12, 2026 · 3 min read

    Cow's Milk Protein Allergy took us by surprise. Here's what we learned and what we wish someone had told us.

    Cow’s Milk Protein Allergy (CMPA) affects around 2–3% of infants, yet it took us months to get a diagnosis. This is our story.

    Early Signs We Missed

    Looking back, the signs were there — but they’re easy to attribute to normal newborn fussiness:

    • Persistent reflux despite treatment
    • Eczema that wouldn’t clear up
    • Blood in stool (the moment we knew something was wrong)

    Getting the Diagnosis

    Our GP was helpful once we came in with a list of symptoms. The elimination diet was hard but transformative.
